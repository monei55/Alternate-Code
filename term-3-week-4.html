Spelling Generalisation: The Doubling Rule

Use the doubling rule when:
  1. the suffix begins with a vowel, and
  2. the base word ends in a short-vowel CVC pattern.

Then double the final consonant before adding the suffix.

slip + ing → slipping
walk + ed → walked (do not double)

Choose the correctly spelt word.

Which word does not need its final consonant doubled?

Checkpoint 10

Compound and Complex Sentences

Compound sentence
Joins two complete ideas using a coordinating conjunction such as and, but, or, so.
Complex sentence
Contains a main clause and a dependent clause, often beginning with because, although, when, while.

Make a compound sentence.

He was tired. He had a big test.

Make a complex sentence using all three ideas.

He was tired. He had stayed up late. He had a big test.
